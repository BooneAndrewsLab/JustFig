# 50 Identity: the family logo and line icons

2026-10-10. Step 1 and the tool-icon part of step 4 of
`DESIGN.md`, the plan for one visual family with PlasmidPop and BarelySig.

## What

- A logo in the siblings' grammar: a lowercase "j" mark whose dot is a
  legend swatch pair (ink + red), standing in for the first letter of an
  outlined Archivo "ustfig" wordmark. Geometry and files in
  `design/logo/README.md`; the brief that produced it in `design/brief/`.
- The lockup replaces the bold text brand in the top bar (`src/ui/Logo.tsx`,
  26 px, ink via `currentColor`). The loading screen shows the mark at 56 px
  in the brand ink on the brand ground. `BRAND.themeColor` and
  `backgroundColor` are the family ink and ground.
- `public/favicon.svg` is the heavier favicon cut, with the ink flipping
  under `prefers-color-scheme`. PWA icons are PNGs at 192, 512 and
  maskable 512, regenerated by `scripts/make-icons.sh` with rsvg-convert,
  as in PlasmidPop; the manifest lists them instead of the SVG favicon.
- The tool palette and the undo, redo, zoom, tab-close and new-tab buttons
  use 16-unit line icons (`src/ui/icons.tsx`: stroke 1.4, round caps,
  `currentColor`, one path each) instead of Unicode glyphs. Drawn for this
  app, like PlasmidPop's sidebar icons, so no icon-set licence ships. The
  word stays in the tooltip and the aria-label; `tools.ts` lost its `glyph`.

## Why

The three apps are built the same way and should look like one set. The
siblings' logos had a defined grammar (`design/logo/README.md` in each
repo), so JustFig's was briefed against it rather than designed alone. The
old mark (four Okabe-Ito shapes on a dark square) was a figure-palette
reference, and the Okabe-Ito colours stay inside the app as the default
figure palette, where they belong, not in the brand.

Unicode glyphs rendered differently on each platform and the pen (✒) and
panel (⊞) glyphs were guesses; a drawn set is consistent everywhere and
costs nothing to ship.

## What is not changed here

The chrome tokens (ink, ground, accent, radius, type size), the "?" help
button, text Undo/Redo and dark mode are later steps of `DESIGN.md`. The
canvas selection colour stays Okabe-Ito blue.
