# Icons

JustFig comes with a library of thousands of openly licensed icons for biology and medicine, gathered from several sources. They live in the Icons panel on the left. Under the Scientific objects shelf you will find the search box, the category list and the grid of icons.

## Finding an icon

- **Search** looks across the whole library. Type part of a name, such as "mitochondria" or "pipette". The count of matches appears above the results; at most 240 are shown, and the count then ends in "+". Make the search more specific to narrow it.
- **Icon category** lists the categories with the number of icons in each. Pick one to browse it. While you are searching, the category list is switched off, because a search covers every category. JustFig remembers the last category.
- Hover over an icon to see its title and where it comes from, with its licence.
- A search also lists any matching **scientific objects** first, under "Scientific objects · redrawn from settings" and tagged **Adjustable**; the icons follow under "Icons · fixed drawings". Searching "plate", for example, offers the configurable Well plate and Petri dish before the plate icons. See [Scientific objects](08-objects.md).

An icon is a fixed drawing: you can recolour, resize and rotate it, but its shape stays the same. A scientific object is redrawn from settings (wells, lanes, organelles, features), so pick one when you want to change what is drawn, not just its colour.

## Placing an icon

- **Click** an icon to place it in the middle of what you can see. It is about 56 pt on its longer side, and keeps its proportions when you resize it.
- **Drag** an icon onto the canvas to drop it where you want it.

Icons are copied into your figure, so a saved `.jfig` file does not need the library.

## Recolouring

Select an icon and use the **Icon colours** section of the Inspector. Most icons have a few colour parts, such as Primary and Outline; change each with its colour control, which also offers your palette swatches (see [Colours and palettes](09-colours.md)). Shades inside an icon move with the colour you choose, so a recoloured icon keeps its depth. **Reset colours** goes back to the original.

Some icons are drawn with many colours and can only be tinted as a whole; their section has a single colour control.

## Licences and share-alike

Every icon has an open licence, and the library lists them all. Most just ask for a credit, which JustFig writes for you (see [Exporting](12-export.md)).

**Share-alike** icons (CC BY-SA) are different: a figure that adapts one must be shared under the same licence. Because that matters for publishing, JustFig hides them by default. They are marked **SA** in the grid and appear only after you tick **Show share-alike (CC BY-SA) icons** at the foot of the panel. JustFig remembers your choice in this browser. If a figure uses a share-alike icon, the Attribution dialog warns you.

Icons under CC BY and CC BY-SA that you recolour are credited as "modified", as their licences require.

## Icon credits and licences

The link **Icon credits and licences** at the foot of the panel opens a dialog with the library's full credit list, by source, licence and author, including the notices that MIT, BSD and Apache icons must keep. The Attribution dialog has an **Icon library credits** button that opens the same list.

Library details, its version and its sources, are shown at the very bottom of the panel.

## Using icons in the right place

Icons work well as nodes of a pathway: place them, then join them with arrows that attach to them (see [Arrows](03-arrows.md)).
